Our Story & Ambience

Visit Our Restaurant

Welcome to The Velvet Plate in Tema Community 11. Inspired by the warm architectural elegance of KULA and vibrant tropical dining spaces, our restaurant blends modern aesthetics with authentic Ghanaian hospitality. From wood-fired aromas to lush, inviting interiors, we invite you to experience a culinary journey rooted in culture, community, and passion.

Ghanaian Heritage & Fire

Crafting Memories Around the Table

At The Velvet Plate, food is more than sustenance—it is a celebration of identity, warmth, and togetherness. Founded in the heart of Tema Community 11, we source the finest local ingredients from Ghanaian farmers and coastlines to honor indigenous flavors while pushing culinary boundaries.

Whether you join us for a cozy evening, family gathering, or a casual spritz at our bar, every detail is designed to feel generous, refined, and distinctly Ghanaian.

Meet Our Team

Master Chefs

Honoring Ghanaian culinary icons and visionaries who redefine modern African gastronomy.

Chef Elijah Amoo Addo

Heritage Gastronomy & Social Impact

Founder of Food for All Africa, Chef Elijah is a pioneer in sustainable culinary arts, elevating traditional Ghanaian recipes into modern dining classics while driving food security innovation.

Chef Selassie Atadika

Nomadic Dining & Indigenous Grains

Visionary behind Midunu, Chef Selassie champions plant-forward dining and ancient African grains, bringing West African culinary heritage to prestigious international stages.

Chef Binta

West African Heritage & Fonio Craft

Basque Culinary World Prize winner, Chef Fatmata Binta is renowned for her nomadic kitchen dining experiences, celebrating sustainable ancient grains and West African flavors.

Chef Ebenezer Smith

Contemporary Fusion & Live-Fire Grilling

Executive grillmaster bringing live-fire culinary perfection to Tema Community 11, combining smoky open-flame cooking with refined Ghanaian spice blends.
