01Why we’re here

Built around
the table.

The Velvet Plate started with a simple idea: make the kind of place where everyone feels like a regular. We cook over live fire, work with the seasons, and leave room for long conversations.

Our team believes hospitality is in the details: a well-timed pour, a warm welcome, and food that gives you something to talk about on the walk home.
